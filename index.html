Brick Series

Our habitats, brick by brick

We rebuilt every habitat we designed for NASA HUNCH out of standard bricks and plates. Each set has its own box art, a parts list with real part numbers, and a building guide that goes bag by bag.

20sets
168,660pieces in all
1,209building steps
◀ Part of Project ARES: the Lunar Smart Adaptive Habitat
Made from our own modelsEach set starts from one of our Blender files. A script reads a copy of the model and leaves the original alone.
Real partsWalls become bricks and thin details become plates, in standard colours. Every parts list includes part numbers, so you can order the pieces.
Instructions includedEvery guide builds bottom-up, one module per bag. Earlier steps are shown faded so the new pieces stand out.
